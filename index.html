Privacy Policy — n8n Railway

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

n8n Railway is a self-hosted workflow automation service currently hosted on a Contabo VPS. It connects services and processes information according to the workflows configured by its owner.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the configured workflows, the service may process messages, task records, notes, expense records, spreadsheet data, account identifiers, and other information submitted to or exchanged between connected services.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to run automated workflows, transfer data between connected services, organize records, generate summaries, and perform requested actions.

4. Third-Party Services

Depending on the configuration, n8n Railway may connect to Google Sheets, Google Cloud, LINE, Supabase, Contabo, and AI service providers. These services may process information as necessary to perform configured tasks, subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored on the Contabo-hosted n8n server or in connected services and workflow databases. Retention depends on the configuration and the applicable services' retention practices.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no online service or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Access should be restricted to authorized users.

7. Data Sharing

Personal information is not intentionally sold. Information may be transmitted to configured third-party providers when necessary to operate the workflows.

8. Your Choices

Users should avoid submitting information they do not want processed by configured workflows. Requests to access, correct, or delete information should be directed to the service owner or handled through the relevant connected service.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service, hosting provider, integrations, or data-processing practices change.
